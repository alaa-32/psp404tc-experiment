# Participant Information and Consent

**Study Title:** From Citation to Decision: Does Easier Access to Evidence Affect Acceptance of AI-Generated Claims?

**University:** Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU)

### About the Study

This study investigates how university students check AI-generated research statements against published research sources.

### What Will I Do?

You will review eight AI-generated statements based on four published research papers.

For each statement, you will open the research source, examine the evidence, and decide whether you would include the statement in a research summary exactly as written.

The study takes approximately **10–15 minutes**. No specialist knowledge is required.

### Privacy and Data Collection

We will record:

- Your decisions for each statement.
- The time taken to complete each question.
- Your interactions with research sources, such as opening a document.

Your responses will be linked to a randomly generated study ID. We will not ask for your name, student number, nationality, or device type.

Once you complete the study, your responses will be transmitted through **FormSubmit.app** to the research team's designated data collection account. This service may also process technical information needed to deliver and protect submissions.

Access to the research responses will be restricted to authorized research team members and relevant university staff where appropriate. Results may be presented in summary form in academic reports and presentations.

Research data will be retained for **30 days** and then deleted according to the research team's data-management arrangements.

### Voluntary Participation

Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may decline or stop participating at any time without penalty.

If you leave before completing the study, your unfinished responses will not be submitted online. Any locally saved progress can be removed from your device using the study's exit controls.

After submitting your responses, you may request their deletion within the 30-day retention period by emailing Alaa.Mezghiche26@student.xjtlu.edu.cn. Please include your study ID, shown at the end of the study, so that the research team can locate and delete your responses.
