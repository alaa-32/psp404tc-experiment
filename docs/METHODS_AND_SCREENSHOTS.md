# Reproducibility and screenshot plan

Design: between subjects, A standard source vs B guided highlighted source. Eight claims, four papers, each once per half, no paper at both sides of the half boundary. Assignment is independent of language, saved once after practice. jsPsych custom plugin finishes only after a valid manual decision. Primary H1/H2 use participant-level rejection proportions and mean decision times. Seeing a paper twice can cause carryover; retain `previous_paper_view` and report this limitation. Original evidence remains English; do not interpret language subgroup differences causally with this small pilot.

Archive the final lockfile, source commit, approved manifest, claim translations, privately reviewed key, evidence checksums, consent version, pilot checklist and analysis policy before recruitment. Record any changes after piloting. Do not mix preview records with research data.

Screenshot checklist (use test records, never identifiable participant screens): language/welcome in both languages; consent; decline; instructions; fictional practice; A opening page 1; B opening the approved highlighted passage; both one/two-passage variants where applicable; previous/next including economics' final page 2; zoom/pan on phone; decision initially disabled; decision enabled after source and choice; recovery notice; honest API acknowledgment; failure/retry and backup downloads; debrief. Capture 360px, 390px and desktop. Actual B research highlight screenshots remain pending the supplied approved variants. Screenshots are evidence for your own report, not a generated report or fabricated results.
